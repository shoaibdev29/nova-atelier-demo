Our Story

Less Clutter. More Meaning.

NOVA ATELIER began in a small Karachi studio with a simple frustration: the everyday objects around us were either cheap and disposable, or beautiful and unusable. We believed a third option existed.

So we design slowly. Each product is prototyped, lived with and argued over before it earns the NOVA name — if it does not make an ordinary moment noticeably better, it does not ship. What remains is a short catalogue of things made properly, priced honestly in rupees, and built to be kept.
